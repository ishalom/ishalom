The bundled fonts — Nunito, Inter, Playfair Display and Oswald — are each
licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which permits bundling
and redistribution with software.

They are bundled rather than linked because spec §13 requires the app to be
fully functional offline, with no network required. A webfont served from a CDN
would make the typography the one part of the product that stops working on a
plane.
